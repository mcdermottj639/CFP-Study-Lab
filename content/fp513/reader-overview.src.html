Course scope & module map

−

FP513 covers investment vehicles, valuation, risk and return measurement, and portfolio construction. Investment Planning is about 17% of the CFP® exam (CFP Board 2021 Principal Knowledge Topics D.27–D.35) — 17% of the whole exam, not of every practice set.
