Learning Objective 5.1.1

Does the relationship constitute Financial Planning?

🎯What is Financial Planning?

The CFP Board's Code of Ethics and Standards of Conduct ("Code and Standards") defines it as:

Definition"A collaborative process that helps maximize a Client's potential for meeting life goals through Financial Advice that integrates relevant elements of the Client's personal and financial circumstances."

It commonly includes income tax planning, budgeting, insurance planning, asset allocation, and retirement planning. It is a process — not a product.

💬What is Financial Advice?

A communication that would reasonably be viewed as a recommendation for a particular course of action regarding:

  • The development or implementation of a financial plan
  • The value of investing in, purchasing, holding, or selling financial assets
  • Investment policies or strategies, portfolio composition, or the management of financial assets
  • The selection and retention of other persons to provide professional services to the Client

It also includes the exercise of discretionary authority over the financial assets of a Client.

🚫What is not Financial Advice?

A reasonable CFP® professional would not view these as Financial Advice:

Directed orders
Responses to a client's directed order are not advice.
Marketing materials
General promotional content.
General financial education
Educational, not tailored.
General financial communications
Broad, non-individualized.
The customization testThe more customized a planner's communications are to a client's individual situation, the greater the likelihood Financial Advice is being provided.

🔗When does Financial Advice require Financial Planning?

Answering yes to these confirms Financial Planning is being provided:

  • Has the planner agreed to provide or provided Financial Planning?
  • Does the client have a reasonable basis to believe the planner will provide or has provided Financial Planning?
  • Do the Integration Factors, applied to the relevant elements, require the Financial Advice to integrate the client's circumstances?

🧩Relevant Elements & Integration Factors

📶What you owe grows as the relationship deepens

The obligations accumulate. Each higher tier keeps everything below it and adds more.

Learning Objective 5.1.2

The six principles of the Code of Ethics

⚖️The Code of Ethics — must be upheld in all instances

As a CFP® professional, the Code of Ethics must be upheld in all instances and encounters, and the Standards of Conduct that apply at all times must be followed as well. A CFP® professional must:

The six principles of the CFP Board Code of Ethics
#Principle
1Act with honesty, integrity, competence, and diligence.
2Act in the client's best interests.
3Exercise due care.
4Avoid or disclose and manage conflicts of interest.
5Maintain the confidentiality and protect the privacy of client information.
6Act in a manner that reflects positively on the financial planning profession and CFP® certification.
Exam skillEvaluate scenarios by asking which principle was violated — honesty/integrity, best interests, due care, conflicts, confidentiality/privacy, or reflecting positively on the profession.

🤝Duties Owed to Clients

The Standards spell out the duties a CFP® professional owes each client. Highlights:

  • Fiduciary Duty — at all times when providing Financial Advice, act as a fiduciary
  • Integrity · Competence · Diligence
  • Disclose and manage conflicts of interest
  • Sound and objective professional judgment; professionalism; comply with the law
  • Confidentiality and privacy; provide information to the client
  • Duties when communicating with a client and when representing compensation method
  • Duties when recommending/using other persons and technology
  • Refrain from borrowing/lending money and commingling financial assets
Exam trapA CFP® professional must generally not borrow from or lend money to a client, and must not commingle a client's financial assets with their own or the firm's (narrow exceptions apply, e.g., a lender in the ordinary course of business or a family member).

🪜Practice Standards — the 7-step financial planning process

When Financial Advice requires Financial Planning, the CFP® professional must follow the Practice Standards:

🏛️Duties Owed to CFP Board

  • Avoid any adverse conduct
  • Report incidents involving adverse conduct to the CFP Board within 30 days
  • Provide a narrative statement to the CFP Board on reportable matters
  • Cooperate with the CFP Board throughout investigations and disciplinary proceedings
  • Comply with the Terms and Conditions of Certification and License
Memorize the number30 days to report adverse conduct — a classic recall item.

👥Duties to Firms & Subordinates · Prohibition on Circumvention

Use reasonable care when supervising
Supervise persons acting under the CFP® professional's direction.
Comply with the firm's lawful objectives
Follow the firm's lawful policies and directions.
Prohibition on circumvention
May not do indirectly, or through another person, anything the Code and Standards prohibit doing directly.
Exam prepPractice reading a planning scenario and spotting where the planner violated the Code & Standards. Anonymous case histories at the CFP Board site show real conduct that led to sanctions.

🤝The fiduciary duties

Under the CFP Board, at all times when providing Financial Advice the professional owes a fiduciary duty — built on loyalty, care, and following the client's instructions. Kaplan frames the broader fiduciary duties as:

Duty of Loyalty
Place the client's interests first; avoid or disclose & manage conflicts.
Duty of Care
Act with care, skill, prudence & diligence given the client's goals and circumstances.
Duty to Disclose
Disclose material facts and conflicts.
Duty to Diagnose
Properly assess the client's situation.
Duty to Consult
Seek outside expertise when needed.
Duty to Keep Current
Maintain up-to-date knowledge.
Don't confuseThe CFP Board's fiduciary duty has three core components — Duty of Loyalty, Duty of Care, and Duty to Follow Client Instructions. The six above are Kaplan's broader teaching list.

📈The Prudent Investor Rule

  • The standard of prudence applies to any investment as part of the total portfolio (not judged in isolation).
  • The trade-off between risk and return is the fiduciary's central consideration.
  • No categorical restrictions on types of investments, as long as appropriate to the risk/return objectives of the trust.
  • Prudent investing integrates the requirement that fiduciaries diversify their investments.
  • Delegation of trust investment and management functions is permitted, with appropriate safeguards.
Fiduciary
Which is a hallmark of the fiduciary standard rather than the suitability standard?
A. Verbal disclosure · B. Disputes go to arbitration · C. Written disclosure & best-interest benchmark · D. Regulated primarily by FINRA
▸ Tap to reveal answer
✓ C — written disclosure & best-interest benchmark

Fiduciary = principle-based, written disclosure, public courts, and aligning to the client's best interests; regulated by SEC/states/DOL. Suitability is rule-based, verbal disclosure, arbitration, FINRA/states.

Learning Objective 5.2.1

The Fitness Standards

✅Who the Fitness Standards apply to

Character-and-fitness standards for individuals seeking CFP® certification:

Candidates
Individuals seeking initial CFP® certification
PER
Professionals Eligible for Reinstatement

Conduct is sorted into Unacceptable Conduct (always bars) and Presumptive Bar (bars unless the DEC decides otherwise).

⛔Unacceptable Conduct — always bars certification

  • Felony conviction of theft, embezzlement, or other financially based crimes
  • Felony conviction of tax fraud or other tax-related crimes
  • Revocation of a financial professional license (securities rep, broker-dealer, insurance, accountant, investment adviser, financial planner) — unless the revocation is administrative (e.g., not renewing by not paying fees)
  • Felony conviction of any degree of murder or rape
  • Felony conviction of any violent crime within the last five years
Exam trapUnacceptable Conduct is a permanent bar with no petition. Contrast it with Presumptive Bar, which is petition-eligible.

⚠️Presumptive Bar — bars unless the DEC reconsiders

Prevents certification unless the Disciplinary and Ethics Commission (DEC) makes a different determination after review:

  • Two or more personal or business bankruptcies
  • Revocation or suspension of a nonfinancial professional license (e.g., real estate, attorney), unless administrative
  • Suspension of a financial professional license, unless administrative
  • Felony conviction for nonviolent crimes (including perjury) within the last five years
  • Felony conviction of a violent crime other than murder or rape that occurred more than five years ago
Petition pathConduct leading to a presumptive bar is eligible for a petition for reconsideration. The DEC may grant the petition, deny it, or deny but allow the individual to re-apply.
Fitness Standards
Per the Fitness Standards, identify the conduct under which an individual is eligible to submit a petition for reconsideration.
A. Felony conviction for tax fraud · B. Two or more personal/business bankruptcies · C. Felony conviction for a violent crime within the last five years · D. Revocation of a financial professional license
▸ Tap to reveal answer
✓ B — two or more bankruptcies

Two or more bankruptcies is a presumptive bar, which is petition-eligible. A, C, and D are Unacceptable Conduct — an absolute bar with no petition.

Disciplinary & appeals process

📜The Procedural Rules & the key parties

The Procedural Rules outline the disciplinary and appeals rules, processes, and policies — letting the CFP Board enforce when a CFP® professional commits wrongdoing.

Respondent
The CFP® professional under investigation.
Enforcement Counsel
Investigates and prosecutes matters.
Hearing Panel
Hears the matter.
DEC
Disciplinary and Ethics Commission — decides sanctions.
Appeals Committee
Reviews appeals of DEC decisions.

🔍Investigations & sanctions

After investigating a matter, if Enforcement Counsel finds probable cause, they take one of the following actions:

Letter of Dismissal
No further action.
Settlement Offer
Resolve by agreement.
Complaint
Formal charge advances.

While investigatory proceedings advance, the CFP Board may issue an interim suspension. The DEC may issue these sanctions:

Sanctions the DEC may impose (least → most severe)
SanctionSanction
Private CensureRevocation
Public CensureTemporary Bar
SuspensionPermanent Bar

🔁Petition for reinstatement — what the Respondent must do

A Respondent's petition for reinstatement may not proceed unless the Respondent has:

  • Completed the suspension
  • Provided a properly-completed CFP Board Ethics Disclosure Questionnaire
  • Provided a written certification that the Respondent has read, understands, and will comply with the Code and Standards
  • Paid the reinstatement fee and any outstanding costs owed to the CFP Board
  • Otherwise satisfied the CFP Board's certification requirements
Exam trapThe requirement is an Ethics Disclosure Questionnaire — not "a CFP Board verified Ethics CE course." The exam offers the CE-course wording as the false option.
