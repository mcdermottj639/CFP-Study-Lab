💵Payout options

  • Straight life — highest payout; stops at death (no beneficiary)
  • Life w/ period certain — pays for set years (5/10/20) even if annuitant dies
  • Joint & survivor — lowest payout; covers two lives

🔁1035 exchange MISSING FROM IMAGE

  • Life → annuity ✓   Annuity → annuity ✓
  • Life → life ✓   MEC → annuity ✓
  • Annuity → life ✗ never (one-way street)

⚰️Inherited (beneficiary) annuity — no step-up MISSING FROM IMAGE

  • A non-annuitized annuity gets NO step-up in basis at death; gains are ordinary income (IRD)
  • Spouse as sole beneficiary may continue the contract (spousal continuation)
  • Non-spouse beneficiary is generally subject to the 10-year rule

⚠️Traps & edges

TrapAnnuities have no contribution limits and no income phase-outs (unlike IRA/401(k)/Roth) — a tested advantage the image omits.
TrapC-share annuities have no surrender charge but higher ongoing fees; Roth annuity = tax-free (not just deferred).
