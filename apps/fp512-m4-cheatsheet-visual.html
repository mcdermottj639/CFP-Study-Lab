✓ Must-know rules

  • Annuity = protection against living too long (longevity) — the mirror of life insurance.
  • Tax-deferred growth; premiums not deductible (after-tax basis if nonqualified).
  • Payouts: life-only = highest, nothing to heirs → period-certain → joint & survivor = lowest.
  • Variable = a security (prospectus + FINRA); fixed = insurance; indexed/EIA = NOT a security.

⚠ Traps

  • 1035 from an annuity into life insurance — not allowed (one-way toward annuities).
  • Mixing the exclusion ratio (annuitized) with LIFO (pre-annuitization withdrawals).
  • Annuity death benefit is NOT income-tax-free like life insurance — the gain is taxable.
  • Applying RMDs to a nonqualified annuity during accumulation.

🎯 Exam tips

  • Client fears outliving money → annuity (longevity), never life insurance.
  • 'Max monthly income, no heirs' → straight life annuity.
  • Spot the tax wrapper first (qualified vs nonqualified) — it drives RMD & basis.

📘 Term reference

Fixed annuityGuaranteed minimum rate; the insurer bears market risk. An insurance product.
Variable annuityCash value in subaccounts; owner bears market risk. A security (prospectus/FINRA).
Indexed (EIA/RILA)Return tied to an index via participation rate & cap; EIA floor usually 0%; RILA uses buffers/floors.
QLACDeferred-income annuity in a qualified plan; up to $210,000 (2025); income can start as late as age 85.
Exclusion ratioPortion of each annuitized payment that is tax-free return of basis.
GLWBGuaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit — income for life even if the account hits $0.
Surrender chargeDeclining fee (≈5–25%) for early withdrawal during the surrender period (≈3–10 yrs).
